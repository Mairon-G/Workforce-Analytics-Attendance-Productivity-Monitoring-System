# Workforce Analytics – Attendance & Productivity Monitoring System

An Excel-based Workforce Analytics solution designed to centralize attendance monitoring, schedule adherence, and productivity tracking in contact center operations.

This project addresses the challenge of managing operational data across multiple sources, providing a structured approach to identifying attendance discrepancies, monitoring employee performance, and evaluating workforce compliance.

## Key Features

- **Attendance Monitoring:** Track employee attendance against scheduled working hours.
- **Schedule Adherence:** Identify late arrivals, early departures, absences, and other scheduling discrepancies.
- **Productivity Tracking:** Analyze productive hours against scheduled hours.
- **Exception Management:** Classify operational statuses and attendance exceptions.
- **Weekly Analysis:** Monitor workforce performance across different reporting periods.
- **Calendar Overview:** Provide a centralized view of attendance patterns and operational activity.
- **Data Consolidation:** Structure information from multiple operational datasets for consistent reporting.

## Tools & Technical Skills

- Microsoft Excel (Advanced)
- Power Query (M Language)
- Data Transformation and Consolidation
- Excel Formulas and Lookup Functions
- Pivot Tables and Data Modeling
- Business Logic and KPI Development
- Workforce Management Analytics

## Business Value

The solution demonstrates how operational data can be transformed into actionable insights, helping Workforce Management and Operations teams improve visibility, identify inconsistencies, monitor attendance compliance, and support data-driven decisions.
